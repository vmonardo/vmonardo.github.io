Diffusion: Add Noise, Then Clean It Up

Slide the noise up to watch a shape dissolve into static, then clean it up one small step at a time and watch it come back.
Add noise is the easy, fixed direction.
Clean up is the hard direction a model has to learn.
Colors mark where each point started, so you can see it scatter and return.

Discussion Questions

  • Adding noise is the easy direction. Why is cleaning noise back up the hard direction a model has to learn?
  • Why is it easier to take many small clean-up steps than to jump from full noise to a finished picture in one move?
  • At full noise, the starting points look the same for every shape. So where does the final shape actually come from?
  • In a real image generator, different starting noise gives different pictures. What plays the role of 'the shape' there?
  • If a shape was never in the training data, could the clean-up steps ever rebuild it? Why or why not?

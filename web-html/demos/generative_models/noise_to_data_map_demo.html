Discussion Questions

  • At 0% training the output is just the raw noise. What is the transformation actually doing as you slide training up?
  • Watch the grid: where does it stretch, and where does it fold or pinch? What does that tell you about the data shape?
  • Follow one color from the noise plot to the data plot. Why do nearby noise points usually end up near each other?
  • The noise on the left is always the same simple blob. So where does all the structure on the right come from?
  • In a real model the transformation is learned from data. What is being adjusted while it 'learns'?

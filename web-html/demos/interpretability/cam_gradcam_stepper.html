Use Good Inputs

This demo works best on clear animal photos, especially one prominent mammal, bird, reptile, fish, or insect.
It is much less trustworthy on people, cars, screenshots, diagrams, text-heavy images, abstract art, or crowded scenes with several competing objects.
Good: one prominent animal
Weak: crowded or non-animal image

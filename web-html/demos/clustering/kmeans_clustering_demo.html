Clustering: Grouping by Similarity

Points are grouped by how close they look. First try to see the groups yourself, then reveal what the algorithm found.
Reveal / hide groups: group by eye before trusting the colors.
Number of groups: you choose how many the algorithm looks for.
Stretch the x-axis: change what counts as “close” and watch the groups change.

Discussion Questions

  • Before turning on the colors, how many groups did you see? Did everyone agree?
  • What happens to the groups when you stretch the x-axis? Why should rescaling change the answer?
  • Is there a single correct number of groups here, or does it depend on what you want the grouping for?
  • When you press 'Re-run grouping', do the groups always come out the same? What does that tell you?
  • If two students named these groups differently, who is right?

  1. In the following formula, what can we not say about \(\lambda\)?

\(\mathcal{L} (\theta) = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N (f_{\theta}(x^{(i)}) - y^{(i)})^2 + \lambda \mathcal{R} (\theta) \)

  • If \(\lambda\) is zero, we get empirical error
  • If \(\lambda\) is big, \(\theta \approx 0\)
  • \(\lambda\) controls how much we regularize \(\theta\)
  • As a result of training our model, we find \(\theta\) and \(\lambda\)

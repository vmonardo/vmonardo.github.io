Noise To Data Map

A simple latent noise variable is sampled first, then a parametric map turns that simple noise into a structured data point.
z ~ N(0, I): sample a simple noise vector.
x = gθ(z): pass it through a learned map.
The parameters θ are fixed in this demo, but in a real generative model they would be learned from data.

What To Notice

Latent noise is simple
Map is parametric
Output can look structured

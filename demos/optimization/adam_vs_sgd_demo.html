Adam vs SGD

Both optimizers start from the same point on the same surface. SGD uses one global learning rate. Adam keeps moving averages of the gradient and gradient square, then adapts the coordinate-wise step size.

Comparison Summary

What The Bottom Plot Means

The blue and orange lines show Adam's effective coordinate-wise scale before multiplying by the first-moment estimate. SGD stays at one global learning rate for every direction.
